About Us

History

Founded in 2018, Football Agency Sierra Leone was established with the mission to discover, develop, and promote young football talents across the nation. Over the years, the agency has worked with several clubs, coaches, and academies to give players the opportunity to showcase their skills locally and internationally.

Vision & Mission

Our vision is to become the leading football development agency in West Africa — empowering talented players with the right training, exposure, and guidance to achieve their dreams.
